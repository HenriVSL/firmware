# v0.15.0: key lock

Long * locks the keys; Menu then * unlocks. The log and key test views
take the lock, the test pattern and full-screen messages do not. A
refused key gives a low beep and a "Keys locked" notice; the header shows
LOCK while locked. Defaults: knob, keypad and side keys (with PTT) all
covered, forced lock off, auto lock off.

Not in this image: settings from the codeplug, and keeping the lock
across power-off (both need later steps).

## Before you start

- Antenna or dummy load on: steps 6 and 10 transmit.
- Test frequency 145.450 MHz simplex, Low power.
- Cable in; the log shows every refused key.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash; wait for the log view (Exit held 1 s switches views) | Start-up as v0.14 |
| 2 | Console: `fm rxw 145450`, `fm power low`; `lock status` | `key lock off; covers knob keypad side-keys+PTT, auto 0 s` |
| 3 | Press 5 | Normal key beep |
| 4 | Hold * for 1 s | Two-note falling beep, "Keys locked" notice, LOCK in the header, `ui: keys locked` |
| 5 | Press 5, Exit, Up, PF1, P1; turn the channel knob | Each: low beep and "Keys locked"; nothing else happens |
| 6 | Press PTT | Low beep, `ui: PTT refused: keys locked`, no `fm: transmitting` line, no carrier |
| 7 | Menu, then * within 3 s | "* to unlock", then rising beep, "Keys unlocked", LOCK gone |
| 8 | Lock again (hold *); Menu; wait 4 s; * | The late * is refused; still locked |
| 9 | Menu, then * at once | Unlocked |
| 10 | PTT for 2 s | `fm: transmitting 145.450 MHz, power low ...`, then back to receive |
| 11 | Lock (hold *); Menu, then hold * for 2 s. Then, unlocked, Menu and hold * again | Unlocks on the press and stays unlocked; the second Menu and long * do not lock (v0.15.1) |
| 12 | `lock set side off`, lock (hold *), PTT 2 s, PF1 | PTT transmits; PF1 passes; digits still refused. Unlock, then `lock set side on` |
| 13 | `lock auto 10`, wait 10 s without touching anything | Locks by itself with the lock beep and `keys locked (auto lock)`. Unlock; `lock auto 0` |
| 14 | `lock set forced on` | LOCK in the header at once; digits refused; Menu refused (no unlock prompt) |
| 15 | `lock set forced off`, `unlock` | Back to normal |
