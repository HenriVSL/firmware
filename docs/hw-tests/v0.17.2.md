# v0.17.2: FM receiver tuning

The FM transceiver starts with the radio's factory tuning instead of the
chip maker's generic values: soft mute, AGC, receive gain, PA bias,
squelch detectors and the band setting. The values depend on the
transceiver version (S or N) named in the radio information block.

## Before you start

- A weak FM signal that stock firmware still receives clearly, such as a
  distant repeater, plus a second radio running stock firmware.
- A wide (25 kHz) and a narrow (12.5 kHz) FM channel, one of them with a
  CTCSS tone.
- USB unplugged while listening: the cable in the accessory jack cuts the
  speaker.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash; watch the start screen and log | "DeltaMM 0.17.2"; the log names the transceiver version (`transceiver tuning for the N version` or `S version`) |
| 2 | Listen to the weak signal on a wide channel next to the stock radio | Audio as clear as on stock, without dropouts while the squelch stays open |
| 3 | Same on a narrow channel | As on stock |
| 4 | A channel with a CTCSS tone: receive a station sending the tone | The squelch opens and audio passes |
| 5 | PTT and talk | The other station hears clear audio at normal loudness |
| 6 | Change channel a few times and come back | Reception stays as in step 2 |
