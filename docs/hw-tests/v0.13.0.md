# v0.13.0: FM receive

The FM transceiver is set up from the radio's own calibration (reference
trim, front-end tuning, squelch threshold) and the baseband runs in analog
mode with the calibrated gains. The squelch opens the speaker. Nothing in
this image can transmit: the power-amplifier control stays at 0 and both
transmit enables stay low.

Frequencies are typed in kHz. Receive works in 136-174 MHz and 400-520 MHz,
the bands the calibration covers.

## Before you start

- v0.12.1 passed steps 1-7, and the verified full backup from v0.2.0 is
  still in the backup folder.
- The USB cable in the jack cuts the speaker: type a command with the cable
  in, then unplug it to listen. The log keeps recording across replugs.
- Pick a UHF frequency that is normally quiet, and know your repeater's
  output frequency if you want to try step 7.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash, keep the cable in, open the log port | `fm: transceiver found; microphone muted, idle (pins 0000 -> 8ae2)`, `calibration: copy A, ...`, `baseband: running: ...` |
| 2 | `fm rx 433500` (or your quiet frequency) | `fm: receiving 433.500 MHz, 12.5 kHz, squelch 3 (opens at -N dBm)`; no warning from the baseband |
| 3 | `fm status` twice, once with the antenna on and once with it off | RSSI in dBm, higher with the antenna on; carrier no |
| 4 | `fm sq 0`, unplug, volume knob to the middle | Steady hiss from the speaker. Blow into the microphone: note whether you hear it through the hiss |
| 5 | Turn the volume knob down and up | The hiss follows the knob |
| 6 | Plug in, `fm sq 3`, unplug | Silence (squelch closed) |
| 7 | Plug in, `fm rx <repeater output kHz>`, unplug and wait for traffic or the repeater's ID | The speaker opens on the signal and closes after it; `fm: squelch open, RSSI ...` and `fm: squelch closed` in the log |
| 8 | Plug in, `fm rxw 145500`, `fm sq 0`, unplug | Hiss on VHF too (25 kHz channel) |
| 9 | Plug in, `fm off` | `fm: idle`; silence |

## If something is off

- No hiss in step 4: the baseband may need more than analog mode and the
  carrier flag before it passes audio. Report the log lines from step 2.
- Hiss very faint or very loud: the transceiver's receive level can be
  changed live with `fm 44 <value>` (hex).
- Your voice heard in step 4: note how loud it is compared with the hiss.

## Report back

- Step 3: the two RSSI values.
- Steps 4-8: hiss heard or not, its loudness, and anything in step 7.
