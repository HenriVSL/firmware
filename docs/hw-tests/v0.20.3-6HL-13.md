# 0.20.3-6HL-13: what to try

A trial build from the `trial` branch: `plug-edit` (the codeplug editor,
`prog watch`) plus the fixes made for the open pull requests. This page
lists everything waiting for a run on the radio, in a sensible order;
each item names the checklist that holds its steps.

## Before you start

- A verified backup (`dmm-radio check-backup`) and the codeplug saved with
  QDMR.
- `dmm-radio` from this build: the radio now shows the factory USB IDs,
  and older `dmm-radio` builds take it for a radio on its factory
  firmware.
- The log port open (`scripts/watchlog.py` finds it by name, as before).

## 1. Flash and the new USB identity

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash this build | It starts; `DeltaMM 0.20.3-6HL-13` in the log |
| 2 | `lsusb -d 28e9:018a -v`, `dmm-radio ports` | Serial `DeltaMM`; two ports, "DeltaMM data" and "DeltaMM log"; `dmm-radio ports` lists both as DeltaMM, none as factory firmware |
| 3 | An unchanged QDMR: `dmrconf detect`, then a read on the data port | Found as a D878UVII; the read as in `programming.md` step 2 |
| 4 | The qdmr fork (its new detection, not committed yet) | Only the data port is tried; read and write work |
| 5 | The CPS under Wine | Unchanged: it uses the mapped COM port |
| 6 | `dmm-radio flash --wait` with this build again | The radio running DeltaMM is not taken for one in update mode; after the switch to update mode the flash goes through |

Steps 8 and 9 of `programming.md` hold the same checks for the pull
request.

## 2. Codeplug editor (first run)

`plug-editor.md`, all 15 steps, then the stack: every task above 50
unused stack words in the latest uptime line. Test channel #16 may be
changed freely.

## 3. Menu and screens

`menu.md` and `screens.md`: the menu drawn in place (no flashing), Up
raises and Down lowers a value, the battery icon.

## 4. APRS

`aprs.md` step 10: with the APRS frequency cleared in the codeplug, no
beacon goes out and the radio says `No APRS frequency`. Put the
frequency back afterwards. Steps 1 and 2 again with it back in place.

## 5. Roaming

From `roaming.md`:

- Steps 4 to 9, 13 and 14 (still open in the pull request).
- With a carrier alone no longer counting as the repeater heard: step 9
  (automatic search after the quiet time) and step 21 (repeater off:
  misses, then searches).
- Beacon timing for any period and length: steps 18, 19, 21 and 22 with
  the hotspot's usual beacons (60 s, 3 s), then again with long ones,
  such as every 120 s for 8 s. Expect each beacon taken (`beacon of
  repeater N, ... ms` up to 16.5 s), the period learned as 120 s, and
  Roam status keeping the level fresh between beacons. Sweeps may take
  longer to find a 120 s repeater the first time.

## 6. Naming codeplug fields with the CPS

The session planned in `docs/plug-edit-status.md`: `scripts/watchlog.py`
logs what each CPS write changes (`prog watch on`); one setting per
round.
