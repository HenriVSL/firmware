# v0.8.0: battery and auto power-off

The screen header shows the battery voltage and four bars; a low battery
turns them red and blinks the red LED. Readings are not calibrated yet
(each radio's offset is in its calibration data, read in a later step), so
the voltage may be off by up to about 1 V, and an empty battery is only
logged: the radio does not switch itself off for it.

## Before you start

- v0.7.0 passed on this radio.
- A multimeter, and ideally a bench supply set to 7.8 V that can go down
  to 5.8 V (in place of the battery, observing polarity).
- A terminal on the log port.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash, power on with a charged battery | Header: voltage and bars at the top right; log `battery: x.xx V, level n (uncalibrated)` |
| 2 | Measure the battery with the meter; type `battery` | Note both values; the difference is the calibration offset |
| 3 | Bench supply: lower from 7.8 V in 0.1 V steps, a few seconds each | Bars drop at about 7.45, 6.85 and 6.45 V (plus the offset); one bar at a time |
| 4 | Go below 6.00 V | `battery: low, please charge`, red voltage and bar, red LED blinks |
| 5 | Back up to 6.2 V | `no longer low`, red LED stops |
| 6 | Down to 5.9 V for 15 s | `below the empty level, but not switching off`; the radio stays on |
| 7 | `autooff 1`, then leave the radio alone | After 1 minute: "Switching off" on screen, then dark; log says so |
| 8 | Turn the power knob off and on | Normal start |
| 9 | `autooff 1`, then press a key every 30 s for 2 minutes | No switch-off while keys are used |
| 10 | Flash factory V3.06 back | The factory firmware works |

## Report back

- Meter and radio readings from step 2, and the voltages where the bars
  changed in step 3.
- The radio's current draw in step 1 and after step 7 (dark), if you have
  a bench supply.
