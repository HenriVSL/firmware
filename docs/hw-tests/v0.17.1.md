# v0.17.1: FM transmit audio

FM transmit audio takes the transceiver's own microphone input only; the
route through the baseband is off. The console can switch routes for
comparison: `fm txaudio direct` (default), `fm txaudio bb` (through the
baseband only) or `fm txaudio both`. The transmit log line names the
route.

## Before you start

- An FM channel and a second station to judge the audio.
- USB unplugged while listening: the cable in the accessory jack cuts the
  speaker.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash; watch the start screen | "DeltaMM 0.17.1" |
| 2 | PTT and talk | The log line ends `audio straight to the transceiver only`; the other station hears clear audio, without a hollow sound |
| 3 | `fm txaudio both`, then PTT and talk | The other station hears a hollow sound |
| 4 | Restart the radio, PTT and talk | Clear audio again |

## Results

2026-10-21: all steps pass. Step 3 is from 0.17.0, which used both
routes: the other station heard a hollow sound. With `direct`, chosen on
the console and then as the default after a fresh start, the audio was
clear.
