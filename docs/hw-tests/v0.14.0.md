# v0.14.0: FM transmit

PTT transmits on the current FM channel and releasing it returns to
receive. Power, deviation and the reference trim come from the radio's own
calibration; the transceiver uses its own transmit drive settings. The
codec connects the microphone only while transmitting.

Limits: transmit only in 144-146 MHz and 430-440 MHz, a timeout (30 s by
default), Low power until another level is chosen, and any other radio
command ends a transmission first.

## Before you start

- An antenna or a dummy load on the radio before every PTT press.
- The SDR tuned to the transmit frequency, to see the carrier, hear the
  audio and check the tone.
- Keep the first transmissions short (2-3 s).
- The cable can stay in: transmit does not need the speaker.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash, open the log port | Start-up as v0.13, no `codec trims not applied` warning |
| 2 | `fm rxw 145600`, `fm tx 145000`, `fm ctcss 885`, `fm power low` | One log line for each setting |
| 3 | PTT for 2 s, saying a few words, then release | `fm: transmitting 145.000 MHz, power low (control N), tone 88.5 Hz, timeout 30 s`, then `transmit off (PTT released) after 2.x s` and receive again. SDR: a carrier on 145.000 MHz while PTT is held, nothing after |
| 4 | Listen to step 3 on the SDR | Voice clear, not distorted or very quiet; the 88.5 Hz tone present (SDR's CTCSS decoder or a low hum) |
| 5 | PTT again for 2 s | The repeater opens: its output on 145.600 MHz carries your voice (SDR) and its tail or ID follows |
| 6 | `fm tot 5`, hold PTT for 8 s | `transmit off (timeout) after 5.0 s`; carrier gone on the SDR at 5 s |
| 7 | `fm tot 30`, then `fm power mid`, `fm power high`, `fm power turbo`, 2 s PTT each | Control value rises step by step; the SDR shows a stronger signal each time. Feel the radio's back: warm is fine, hot is not |
| 8 | `fm tx 147000`, PTT | `PTT: 147.000 MHz is outside the 2 m and 70 cm bands`; no carrier |
| 9 | `fm rx 433500`, `fm tx off`, `fm ctcss off`, `fm power low`, PTT 2 s | Carrier on 433.500 MHz on the SDR (simplex, 12.5 kHz) |
| 10 | Flash factory V3.06 back; check FM and DMR and the codeplug | Works as before |

## If something is off

- No carrier at all, but the log says transmitting: report the log; check
  `fm 30` (should read 4046 or 7046) and `fm 1f`.
- Carrier but no audio, or very quiet audio: the microphone path through
  the codec or the transceiver's input. Report; `codec 0 41 <value>` and
  `fm 44 <value>` change the levels live.
- Audio distorted or too wide: `fm 59` shows the deviation register.
- PTT does nothing: check that `fm rxw` ran first.

## Report back

- Steps 3-5: carrier, audio quality, tone, repeater opened or not.
- Step 7: what the SDR shows per level, and how warm the radio got.
