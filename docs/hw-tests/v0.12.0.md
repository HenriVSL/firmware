# v0.12.0: audio out (beeps and volume)

A new audio task owns the speaker. Key presses beep, with the beep made by
the MCU itself (a PWM tone on PB9), not by the DMR chip. The volume knob
sets both the beep level and the codec's receive-audio level. Receive
audio and beeps share the amplifier: a beep cuts receive audio while it
plays. The step-8 test keys are gone.

The microphone stays muted throughout (FM chip GPIO3 high).

## Before you start

- v0.11.1 passed on this radio, and the verified full backup from v0.2.0 is
  still in the backup folder.
- The USB cable in the jack cuts the speaker: listen with it unplugged.

## Steps

| # | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Flash, keep the cable in, open the log port | `audio: volume N`, `fm: transceiver found; microphone muted`, `baseband: running: ...` |
| 2 | Turn the volume knob up and down | `audio: volume` lines from 0 (fully down) to 15 (fully up), and `baseband: speaker volume N: set` |
| 3 | Unplug the cable; volume knob to the middle; press a few keys | A short 800 Hz beep on every key press (not on PTT). Note any pop or click before or after it |
| 4 | Turn the knob down step by step, pressing a key each time | Beeps get quieter; fully down (step 0) is silent |
| 5 | Knob fully up, press a key | Loud but not harsh; note if it distorts |
| 6 | Hold a key | One beep only, at the press |
| 7 | Plug the cable in: `beep 1000 300` | `audio: tone 1000 Hz, 300 ms at level N` |
| 8 | `rx on`, unplug, talk near the radio, then plug in and `rx off` | With the speaker on for receive audio, your voice does not come back (microphone muted) |
| 9 | Flash factory V3.06 back; check FM and DMR and the codeplug | Works as before |

## If something is off

- No beep at all, but `audio:` lines appear: the PB9 tone path may need the
  receive switch (PE0) too. Report it; `rx on` then a key press tests that.
- A loud pop before the beep: the amplifier settle time (150 ms) may be
  too short, or the pop comes from PB9 itself. Report when it happens.
- Beep too quiet at full volume: report; the loudest level is a guess.
